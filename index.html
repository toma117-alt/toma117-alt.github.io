01 / About

Powering ideas, one watt at a time

I'm an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate from BUET with a strong foundation in renewable energy systems, power-system analysis, optimization, embedded systems and electrical engineering.

I'm experienced in MATLAB/Simulink-based simulation, power-system studies, hardware implementation and interdisciplinary engineering projects.
